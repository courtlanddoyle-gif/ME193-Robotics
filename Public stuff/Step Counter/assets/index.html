How accurate is this? (uncertainty analysis)

The ± next to each number is a 90% range: in testing, 9 out of 10 walks were off by less than that. Every test walk was judged by a model that had never seen it.

Limitations

  • The ±11% comes from short walks (10–75 steps); most error is at the start and stop, so long walks are usually closer.
  • Calibrated to one person. Someone else's legs and gait would need new recordings.
  • The IMU must be strapped on the inside of the right ankle the same way every time.
  • Distance has no GPS or map: errors add up and never get corrected. Not tested on stairs or hills.
  • After you stop, the count keeps catching up for about 2 s (each step is counted over a 2 s window).
  • Power up with the sensor still: the gyro calibrates in the first 2 s.
